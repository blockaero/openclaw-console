OpenClaw read-only ARM smoke

This site is the published copy of the plan and the local scaffold. It does not connect to ARM, does not mint a key, and does not send tools/list or tools/call.

The smoke stays blocked until ARM ships a real read-only level. initialize may write one server-generated last-seen timestamp, and nothing else. The production ARM URL is refused by the scaffold.
